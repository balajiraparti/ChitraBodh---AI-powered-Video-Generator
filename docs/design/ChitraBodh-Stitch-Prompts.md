These contain brand/product context only, never credentials or private project data. No direction is selected yet.

## Shared brief — paste first

Design a brand-direction board for ChitraBodh, an educational motion-explainer video product. Its name connects pictures and understanding. The philosophy is “Ideas in motion. Made clear.” Audience: creators and educators; initial product is for an invited internal team. The creator enters an idea, AI creates script/storyboard/narration and meaningful diagrams, then the creator previews and shares. Keep the experience simple, thoughtful, creative, professional and modern.

For this pass, create a visual brand board, NOT a full dashboard or production homepage. Show palette with hex values, typography specimens, a small wordmark concept, one real-looking button/input specimen, and an explanatory diagram. Label diagrams as design specimens. Provide enough whitespace to assess the system. Create original composition, not a generic SaaS template. Do not include pricing, analytics, fake metrics, logos/testimonials, stock robots, sparkles, gradient blobs, glassmorphism or irrelevant3D art. No simulated working video playback. The exported video itself is a linear MP4, not an interactive lesson. Direction is exploratory, not approved.

Use a clear accessible hierarchy, readable contrast, mobile-friendly controls and concise copy. Do not reduce important text to tiny uppercase labels. Use “Make ideas make sense.” as display copy and “Turn a topic into a clear, narrated explainer.” as supporting text. Control specimens can say “Create video” and “Explain the water cycle.”

## A — Daylight Studio

Explore warm editorial creativity with technical precision. Background #F7F5EF, ink #202B27, muted text #59665E, primary action #126B58 with white label. Illustration highlights #F2C14E and small #E77B5B accents. Functional white surfaces. Light divider #D9DED6; control boundary #7C887F. Use Manrope for main type/UI, with Instrument Serif italic on only one short editorial phrase. Moderately soft10px controls and16px media panels. Asymmetric composition and generous whitespace. Avoid a decorative stationery aesthetic.

Show an original sun/hill/water diagram using precise paths, one yellow sun and restrained directional arrows. Use an open frame plus dot as a provisional symbol for an idea finding clarity; keep the ChitraBodh wordmark highly legible. Warmth should come from canvas and type, not excessive textures. It must still feel like a capable modern product. This is the recommended candidate but not an approved direction.

## B — Cobalt Lab

Explore a crisp design laboratory. Background #F3F5F9, ink #18243E, muted #55617A, primary #3155D9 with white labels. Small highlight #C5E76B, supporting illustration #9DADEB. White surfaces, divider #D8DEEA, control boundary #79859D. Space Grotesk headlines and Manrope UI/body. Controls8px/panels12px. Strong alignment, editorial grid, short numbered annotations. Do not make it a dense developer console.

Create a clear linear-search diagram using array boxes [4,7,2,9], target2 and a current index pointer. Show first two checks and the found third element through labels as well as color. Use this as a brand specimen, not a fake generated video. A selected module and a connecting path can become the candidate logo motif. Keep the lime within the diagram and use cobalt purposefully.

## C — Afterlight

Explore a cinematic yet restrained dark creative studio. Graphite #141C24, raised surface #202C37, main text #F0F4EE, muted #A9B5B7. Primary #B9F27C with #141C24 text; secondary diagram accent #A6B6FF. Divider #354553 and essential control boundary #70828F. Space Grotesk headlines with Manrope body. Controls12px/media16px. Large negative space, crisp type, no blurry glow, neon outlines, glass panels or cyberpunk noise.

Use a small visual sequence of keyframes connected by a motion path, with one emphasized moment, to suggest narration synchronized with graphics. Keep it recognizably educational; no fake video-editor timeline or intimidating controls. A frame-and-path wordmark symbol should stay simple enough for monochrome use. Prioritize text contrast and long-session reading comfort.

## After choosing a direction — next homepage pass

Use only the selected direction's tokens and type. Design one desktop and one mobile product-introduction homepage, preserving the brand board. Include restrained navigation, hero with meaningful teaching visual, three-stage idea/generate/preview flow, honest sample topics and one closing action. Do not invent features, publication readiness, customer numbers, pricing or public signup. Keep working behavior separate from illustration. Do not generate all application screens yet. Show a neutral loading/error/expiry component specimen alongside the page for later dashboard reuse.
