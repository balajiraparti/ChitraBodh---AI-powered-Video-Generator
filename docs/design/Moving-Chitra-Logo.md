# Moving Chitra · Chitrabodh logo

Use the supplied reference as the visual master and [the production symbol](../../dashboard/public/moving-chitra.svg) as the reusable artwork. The shared web header combines this SVG with the live wordmark **Chitrabodh**, capital C and lowercase b. The mark links to the homepage.

## Construction

Three frames ascend from the lower left toward the upper right. The foreground circle introduces the subject, the middle quarter-circle suggests exploration, and the upright gold triangle marks an insight. Preserve the pale folded corner at the top of the rear frame. That fold belongs to the rear frame.

The reference has a wide silhouette. The master therefore uses a 312 × 232 viewBox. Keep this aspect ratio when resizing. Reserve a square tile for the favicon, centering the same silhouette within it. The circle and middle opening reveal the surface underneath. On cream they appear cream, on white they appear white. This resolves the draft’s conflicting instructions for white fill and transparent cutouts.

These are flat vector approximations of the supplied raster. Use the full-color lockup on white or warm cream. On a dark or busy surface, place it on a cream tile with clear space. Use the logo colors within the artwork and the Daylight palette for interface controls.

## Wordmark and sizing

Use the bundled Manrope typeface at weight 800 with tracking of −0.045em. Keep the symbol on the left, vertically centered with the wordmark. At desktop header size use a 54px-wide symbol, 9px gap and 27px text. At narrow phone sizes use 42px, 6px and 22px respectively. Leave at least half a wordmark capital-height clear around the lockup. Use the standalone symbol when the name cannot fit comfortably.

## Web usage

The shared header uses real text for the name and an empty alt attribute on the adjacent decorative symbol, so assistive technology announces the link once. The [favicon](../../dashboard/public/favicon.svg) includes a cream backing to accommodate light and dark browser chrome. Keep the navigation logo static. Stacked, monochrome and animated variants can be developed when a specific placement needs them.
